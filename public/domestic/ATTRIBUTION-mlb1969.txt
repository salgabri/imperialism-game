MLB map of the seasons mlb-war-1969 (public/domestic/mlb1969.json), mlb1969-198388e1
Generated by scripts/build-club-maps.mjs; do not edit.

Territory shapes are derived (dissolved, simplified, quantized) from the administrative
boundary layers below; each layer keeps its own licence.

geoBoundaries (https://www.geoboundaries.org), gbOpen release, each file pinned to the commit in its URL:
  Runfola, D. et al. (2020) geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries. PLOS ONE 15(4): e0231866. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231866
Land the game's world map (public/countries-50m.json: world-atlas 2.0.2, ISC; Natural Earth 1:50m,
  public domain) draws for another country is cut; see "Cut" lines below.
Ballpark locations: scripts/data/mlb-war-seasons.json, every club of each season at the ballpark it played most of its home games in (the Baseball Databank's HomeGames: Chadwick Baseball Bureau, CC BY-SA 3.0, based on the game logs and park codes published by Retrosheet, which are available free of charge from and are copyrighted by Retrosheet, www.retrosheet.org), named as the map's first season knew it (a ballpark renamed in place keeps the map); the names of the day and the coordinates of the ballparks of the 1960s and 1970s from English Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Clubs that played in another club's ballpark grow from their own previous one: the Angels of 1962–1965 (Dodger Stadium) from Wrigley Field, Los Angeles, and the Yankees of 1974–1975 (Shea Stadium) from Yankee Stadium.
