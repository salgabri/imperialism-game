League of Legends seasons 2016-2025 (lol-<year>.json): attribution
===========================================================

The ratings in these files are computed by this project from the match results gol.gg (Games of Legends, https://gol.gg)
records: an Elo of every player, built game by game from the leagues and the international events of each year and the year
before, each newcomer starting at his league's level, as it stood on the last day of the year. gol.gg publishes no ratings:
these are not its numbers. Each player's country is the one gol.gg files him under, or, for players it files under none,
the one esportsearnings.com or Wikidata does. See docs/design/league-of-legends.md, "Earlier seasons".

Credit: gol.gg (https://gol.gg); each player's source links his page there.
Countries gol.gg lacks: esportsearnings.com (https://www.esportsearnings.com) and Wikidata (https://www.wikidata.org, CC0).
