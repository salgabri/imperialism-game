Their ratings are computed by this project, not a game's: each finalist's strength from its Elo the day after the
final, its eleven the players of its World Cup squad with the most minutes, ordered by their records
(docs/design/football-years.md, "Computed World Cup seasons"; scripts/data/world-cup-seasons.json).
