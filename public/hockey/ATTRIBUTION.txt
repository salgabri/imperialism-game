Ice hockey seasons (public/hockey/nhl-<year>.json): sources, terms and attribution
=========================================================================

EA's own ratings of a season (1992/93, 1993/94, 1994/95, 2006/07, 2021/22): each file's source names the edition and who compiled it (docs/design/hockey.md, "Earlier seasons").

Converted from EA's attributes (2007/08, 2008/09): EA's NHL 08 and NHL 09 attributes as IGN's team pages published them
(the Wayback Machine's captures, pinned in scripts/data/nhl-seasons-sources.json); the overall is this project's conversion, not EA's
(docs/design/hockey.md, "NHL 08 and NHL 09"). NHL 09's skaters take their positions from the Hockey Databank's rosters (2008/09's,
else the seasons beside it, else NHL 08's pages), and a player EA's Country does not place takes the Hockey Databank's birth
country, under its terms and statement below.

Computed from the players' season statistics by this project's rule (docs/design/hockey.md, "Computed seasons"). These are not any game's ratings.

- 1960/61, 1961/62, 1962/63, 1963/64, 1964/65, 1965/66, 1966/67, 1967/68, 1968/69, 1969/70, 1970/71, 1971/72, 1972/73, 1973/74, 1974/75, 1975/76, 1976/77, 1977/78, 1978/79, 1979/80, 1980/81, 1981/82, 1982/83, 1983/84, 1984/85, 1985/86, 1986/87, 1987/88, 1988/89, 1989/90, 1990/91, 1991/92, 1995/96, 1996/97, 1997/98, 1998/99, 1999/00, 2000/01, 2001/02, 2002/03, 2003/04, 2005/06, 2009/10: the Hockey Databank, version 13.0 of 10 July 2018 (maintained through the hockey-databank Yahoo group), as published at github.com/rippinrobr/hockey-databank.
  Terms: free use of the data, a commercial product included, with the Hockey Databank's attribution statement displayed in its entirety (its readme). The statement it asks for:

  "The information used herein was obtained free of charge from and is copyrighted by the Hockey Databank project.  For more information about the Hockey Databank project please visit http://sports.groups.yahoo.com/group/hockey-databank"

- 2010/11, 2011/12, 2012/13, 2013/14, 2014/15, 2015/16, 2016/17, 2017/18, 2018/19, 2019/20, 2020/21, 2022/23, 2023/24, 2024/25, 2025/26: MoneyPuck.com's season summaries and game-by-game player data (moneypuck.com/data.htm).
  Terms: free to use for non-commercial purposes, crediting MoneyPuck.com (moneypuck.com/data.htm). Credit: MoneyPuck.com (https://moneypuck.com).
  Non-commercial: these files may not be used for commercial purposes without MoneyPuck.com's leave.
  Where MoneyPuck's player lookup gives no nationality, a season takes the Hockey Databank's birth country, under its
  terms and statement above (2010/11, 2011/12, 2012/13, 2013/14, 2014/15, 2015/16, 2016/17, 2017/18, 2018/19, 2019/20, 2020/21, 2022/23, 2023/24, 2024/25).

In the game, setup shows the chosen season's credit under the Season choice (docs/design/hockey.md, "Credits"): the Hockey
Databank's statement above, in full, on the 59 seasons that take anything from the databank, and "Data: MoneyPuck.com" on the 15 computed from its data.
